## Winner

**128 characters, 130 bytes** (91% of the 140 budget; the em dash is 3 bytes,
which keeps the Size tie-break small). Mean game score 0.62 — clears the
±0.60 bar; only D15 and D11 cleared it. Why it wins under the brief, in
criterion order: (1) unmistakably a roast of the human operator; (2) the
surprise turn — literal obedience backfires, and the punch ("why none of
them have won yet") is his own impatience quoted back at him; (3) warm
underneath — it is a joke about trust, not contempt; (4) specific to this
operator — his exact phrase, his forty entries, his real impatience;
(5) one concrete scene held to the end, punch on the last words, no throat
clearing, no judge addressed. The Indonesian phrase is glossed by the
sentence itself, so the joke lands cold for a stranger.
